Field Log — Profile

Michael graduated First Class in Agricultural & Environmental Engineering from the Federal University of Technology, Akure — top of a class of 78. His work sits at the intersection of precision agriculture and land-systems engineering: georeferenced field surveying, GIS-based spatial analysis, and the design of low-cost, sustainable irrigation infrastructure.

During an internship at the International Institute of Tropical Agriculture (IITA), he ran RTK-GPS soil surveys and precision land-levelling across a dozen research plots, then translated that field data into irrigation and drainage systems for smallholder soybean fields. He later stepped into site management for a construction firm, coordinating crews, procurement, and quality sign-off on live builds.

He's now looking for research, project-implementation, or graduate-study opportunities that put his analytical and field-management skills to work.
